01The problem

UWorld’s business rests on a simple promise: the right people get the right content, for the right product, at the right price. In an AI-accelerated threat era that promise is hard to keep — adversaries no longer need deep skill, only a prompt — and today we find most abuse the slow way: by hand, across scattered logs, days after the damage is done.

02How it works

A modular monolith — no new microservice fleet. A .NET BackgroundService ingests signals, a deterministic rule engine flags expected-vs-actual deviations into RiskCases, and an AI copilot explains each case. Humans decide; the existing .NET authorization logic stays the single source of truth.

03The detection rules

Deterministic rules define expected behaviour. Each produces a scored case with linked evidence. Four are built deep; three give broad coverage. Every example below is a real case the seeded demo produces.

AccessCritical

Authorization Gap

Protected content returned even though the correlated authorization outcome was Denied or Missing — separating “request denied” from “content actually returned.”

Demo: AuthResult=Denied yet 54,000 bytes returned for /qbank/package/40/q/1.
● Deep rule · the insight judges remember
EntitlementHigh

Entitlement Leakage

Entitlement evaluated at event-time, not current state — access after expiry, cancellation or refund.

Demo: Entitlement 4004 refunded, yet package 30 accessed 4 days later.
● Deep rule · point-in-time correctness
RevenueHigh

Revenue & Discount Abuse

Unexpected zero-pay purchases, price-below-policy orders, and one discount code reused across linked accounts.

Demo: Code SAVE50 used by accounts 5, 6, 7 (one linked group).
● Deep rule
IPCritical

Content Scraping

Scores volume, rate and sequentiality together to separate harvesting from genuine study.

Demo: 120 sequential questions (1→120) in 4 min at 30/min.
● Deep rule · protects core IP
AccountHigh

Coordinated Account Abuse

Weighted multi-signal scoring (device + discount + timing). Never shared-IP alone.

Demo: Accounts 5, 6, 7 corroborate on 3 signals (shared device + reused discount).
○ Broad coverage
SecurityHigh

CVE Exposure

Matches a disclosed CVE to a live asset running the affected component + version.

○ Broad coverage

04What makes it stand out

Not “another alert tool.” It’s explainable, governed and demonstrably safe — the trust story is the product.

★

Explainability-first

Every flag shows why — cited evidence plus an expected-vs-actual diff, not just a status code.

⏱

Event-time correctness

Entitlements evaluated at the moment of access, not against current state.

⚖

Transparent scoring

Each case shows per-signal weight contributions — never one opaque number, never shared-IP alone.

🛡

Governed AI

A redaction layer strips emails & IPs in code before anything reaches a model. Enforcement stays human-owned.

📜

Full audit trail

Created → AI summary → assigned → escalated → resolved, every step recorded per case.

🔭

Self-observability

The platform emits its own telemetry through the New Relic client — it watches itself.

Responsible by design: The AI only investigates and explains — it never enforces access or changes accounts or payments. Deterministic rules stay the source of truth; a human always decides. No sensitive data is sent to the model, and every case keeps an auditable trail. Defending the AI era with AI, responsibly.

05The 90-second demo

  1. Seed & ingest. The worker turns synthetic SQL/New Relic data into 13 evidence-backed cases across all 7 rules.
  2. Open the queue. Cases ranked by score & severity — Critical authorization gaps and scraping at the top.
  3. Investigate a case. Evidence timeline + expected-vs-actual: “Denied, yet 54,000 bytes returned.”
  4. Ask the copilot. Cited summary, labelled uncertainty, recommended next checks — advisory only.
  5. Resolve it. Assign → escalate → resolve; the audit trail records who did what.
  6. Simulate live. One click spawns a brand-new Critical case on stage.

Value to UWorld

  • Protects revenue — catches payment/entitlement mismatches and discount abuse before they compound.
  • Protects core IP — detects scraping of the question banks that are the product.
  • Finds exposure faster — real gaps vs. noise; new CVEs mapped to live assets.
  • Saves analyst hours — correlates SQL + New Relic timelines into a reviewed case.
  • Shortens release diagnosis — denial/payment spikes caught right after deploy.
  • Builds trust & stays safe — deterministic, human-approved, fully auditable.

Net effect: less revenue leakage, stronger protection of content and students, faster and more confident investigations — all on the existing stack.
