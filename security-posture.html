USpark 2026 · Security posture & threat model

“What if they attack the database, take us down, or use AI to hack us non-stop?”

No single app stops every attack — real security is layered. Here’s the honest picture: what the infrastructure owns, what this platform adds as the detection & investigation layer, and why it’s built so it never widens the attack surface.

01Shared responsibility — where this platform sits

Straight answer: this platform is not a firewall, a DDoS shield, or a database. It doesn’t replace network/infra security — it’s the layer that sees the attack early, explains it, and routes it to a human, while being engineered not to add new risk of its own. Defence in depth, each layer doing its job:

Perimeter / EdgeInfra owns
WAF, CDN, DDoS protection, rate limiting, bot management prevents volumetric + L7 floods
NetworkInfra owns
Segmentation, private endpoints, zero-trust, egress control limits blast radius
ApplicationEng owns
AuthN/AuthZ, input validation, parameterized queries, secrets management closes app vulns
DataInfra owns
Least-privilege DB accounts, encryption at rest/in transit, backups, immutable audit logs protects + recovers data
Detection & Investigation★ This platform
Correlates SQL + New Relic signals into evidence-backed cases: exfiltration-like reads, denial/error spikes, CVE exposure, automated probing — in seconds. sees it early, explains it
Human response★ Workspace
Analyst triages, assigns, escalates, resolves — with a full audit trail. The AI advises; the human + deterministic .NET authz act. decides & acts

03The headline threat: AI attacking us continuously

The proposal’s own premise: in an AI-accelerated era, adversaries no longer need deep skill — only a prompt — and they probe continuously, at machine speed. A human-paced, after-the-fact review loses that race. This platform is the counter.

∞

Always-on, machine-speed

A .NET BackgroundService ingests and evaluates on a loop — 24/7, no coffee breaks. Continuous attacks meet continuous detection.

⟳

Idempotent, no alert fatigue

Deterministic, versioned rules with a dedup key mean the same probing doesn’t spawn duplicate noise — repeated automated attempts converge to one evolving case.

◎

Built to spot machines

The very signals that betray automation — sequentiality, rate, multi-account coordination — are what the rules score. Bot-like behaviour is what we’re tuned for.

⚖

AI vs AI, responsibly

An AI copilot compresses hours of correlation into seconds so defenders keep pace — but it only explains. Enforcement stays deterministic and human-approved. We fight AI with AI without handing it the keys.

04Secure by design — we don’t widen the attack surface

A security tool that becomes a new way in is a liability. This one is built to be a hard target and a quiet guest.

R

Read-only, least privilege

The platform only reads source signals; it never writes back to orders, payments, entitlements or access. A compromised copilot has nothing to change.

¶

No injectable queries

100% parameterized ADO.NET + stored procedures — no string-built SQL, so the tool itself can’t be SQL-injected.

∅

No secrets to the model

A redaction layer strips emails & IPs before anything reaches the AI; the model has no credentials and no network to the DB.

📜

Everything audited

Every request, case, action and AI call is logged with who/what/when/cost — tamper-evident evidence for incident response and compliance.

05How security helps the business, not just the system

Security that pays for itself

• Protects revenue — catches leakage, zero-pay and discount abuse before it compounds (quantified in dollars per case).

• Protects the crown-jewel IP — detects scraping of the question banks that are the product.

• Shrinks time-to-detect — hours of manual log-digging become a reviewed case in seconds, so the risk window closes while it’s still open.

• Defends the SLAs — denial/regression spikes caught right after deploy keep contractual uptime promises.

• Earns trust — provable governance (no data to the model, human-approved enforcement, full audit) is a B2B sales asset, not just a control.

Net: less revenue leakage, stronger IP & student protection, faster and more confident response — on the existing stack.
