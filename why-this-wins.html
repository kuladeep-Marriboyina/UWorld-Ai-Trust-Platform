01Eight reasons, each with proof

Every claim below is backed by something already running in the prototype — not a promise.

1

It protects the exact thing the business is

UWorld’s promise is “the right people get the right content, for the right product, at the right price.” This platform is a direct guard on that sentence — access, entitlements, pricing, and the question banks that are the product. Most projects improve something adjacent to the business; this defends its core.

Rules map 1:1 to access, entitlement, revenue and IP protection
2

It makes security measurable in dollars — proven, not promised

The rare security project with a built-in ROI meter. Every case carries an estimated revenue-at-risk; every AI investigation carries a token cost; both roll up by request, rule and day. You walk into a budget meeting with a number, not a vibe.

One pass: $2,503 at risk vs $0.0056 AI spend = ~443,000× — measured, not estimated

3

It’s aimed where 80% of the money is — B2B

B2B is where risk concentrates (one contract = 100–1000× a consumer seat) and where the crown-jewel IP is most exposed to partners and resellers. The platform turns each leak into per-tenant renewal intelligence and its governance helps close institutional deals by passing their security reviews. Security becomes a revenue function.

Per-tenant risk → “Contract #4471 leaking $38k via seat-sharing → upsell at renewal”
4

Hours → seconds, with evidence people actually trust

Today abuse is found by hand, days late, across scattered SQL and New Relic logs. This replaces that with evidence-backed cases showing expected-vs-actual and cited evidence — not black-box alerts. The “denied, yet content actually returned” insight and the event-time entitlement check are things a status code alone never reveals.

Case detail: evidence timeline + expected-vs-actual + cited AI summary
5

Responsible AI you can defend to a board, a regulator, or a buyer

The AI only investigates and explains — it never enforces. Deterministic rules and the existing .NET authorization stay the single source of truth; no sensitive data reaches the model (redaction in code); every case and AI call is fully audited; and a prompt-injection guard means even “make the AI hack us” has no lever to pull.

Injection guard neutralized 3/3 red-team payloads · redaction + full audit trail verified
advisory-onlyno data to modelhuman-approvedauditable
6

It runs on the stack you already have

.NET 8 + Angular + SQL Server + New Relic, as a modular monolith — no new microservice fleet, raw ADO.NET in the house style. Nothing exotic to buy, hire for, or learn. Cheap to adopt, cheap to own, reuses what the teams already run.

Builds clean on .NET 8; parameterized ADO.NET; reuses existing SQL + New Relic
