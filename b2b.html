The same seven rules, but every number gets bigger and every identity is one of many under a single contract.

Risk is concentrated

One institutional contract can be worth 100–1000× a consumer seat. A single seat-sharing or post-contract-access event is a large-dollar leak — so the business-impact ledger surfaces far bigger figures per case.

◆

Your IP is most exposed to partners

The biggest question-bank scraping threat isn’t a student — it’s a partner institution or reseller harvesting content to build a competitor. It hides inside “normal” per-seat traffic.

⧉

Many identities, one contract

Credential sharing beyond licensed seats is the defining B2B leak. Multi-account / seat-sharing detection becomes the central control, not a side rule.

✓

Security is contractual

B2B SLAs make uptime and security obligations you’ve signed. Detecting exposure and regressions early protects the contract, not just the system.

04Standout #2 — trust that helps you close B2B deals

Institutional buyers run a security review before they sign. The platform’s governance is exactly what their procurement asks for — so it accelerates deals instead of slowing them.

Security as a revenue enabler, not a cost centre

• No sensitive data to the model — a redaction layer strips emails & IPs in code (answers data-handling questions).

• AI is advisory-only — deterministic .NET authorization stays the single source of truth; the model never enforces (answers “is the AI making decisions about our people?”).

• Full audit trail — every case and AI call is logged with who/what/when/cost (answers SOC-2-style evidence requests).

• Per-tenant isolation & attribution — each institution’s risk is tracked and reported separately.

Net: fewer procurement blockers, faster signatures, a differentiator competitors can’t show on a slide.
