03B2B vs B2C — same engine, different shape

The platform handles both because the rules are the same; only the scale and the dollar-size differ.

B2B institutions

  • Few, large contracts — each leak is big-dollar
  • Seat-sharing beyond licensed count is #1
  • Partners/resellers are the top scraping threat
  • Rolls up to per-contract renewal risk
  • Trust = a deal-closer in security reviews

B2C students

  • Many, small transactions — leaks add up at scale
  • Password sharing & free-trial farming are #1
  • Individuals scraping to resell/share
  • Rolls up to total leakage & abuse trends
  • Trust = brand & fairness to honest students

Tuned so real students aren’t annoyed: the scraping rule needs volume + speed + in-order together (a real student browsing a few questions is ignored), and coordinated-account never flags a shared IP alone — because dorms, campuses and home Wi-Fi share IPs legitimately. Low false positives is itself a B2C feature.
